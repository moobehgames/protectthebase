Privacy Policy – Protect the Base
Effective date: 21 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Protect the Base (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published by Moobeh Games ("I", "me"), an independent developer based in the United Kingdom.

Summary
The App does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Information I collect
I do not collect any personal information through the App. This includes names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers, health or fitness data, and financial or payment information.

Any game progress or settings (such as scores) are stored only on your device and are never sent to me. They are deleted when you uninstall the App.

Advertising and analytics
The App does not show advertisements and does not use any advertising or analytics services.

Third-party services
The App is distributed through Google Play. When you download or use apps from Google Play, Google may collect certain information (for example, crash reports or install statistics) under its own privacy policy, which you can read at https://policies.google.com/privacy. I do not control that data and only see anonymous, aggregated statistics that Google provides to developers.

Sharing of information
Because I do not collect personal data, I do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal data with anyone.

Children's privacy
The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information through the App, please contact me and I will take steps to delete it.

Your rights
Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), and similar laws in other regions, you have rights over your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. Since the App does not collect personal data, there is normally nothing for me to access or delete, but you are welcome to contact me with any question. If you are in the UK, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

If you contact me
If you email me, I will receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. I will use it only to reply to you, and will delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

Changes to this policy
If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and change the effective date above.
